# Anime girl

- Source: `source/source.glb` (Sketchfab "3d anime character girl for blender", 5.3 MB). Compressed: `host.glb` (≈ 631 KB).
- VRoid/Unity humanoid skeleton, 185 joints. Names look like `Left arm_20`, which becomes `Left_arm`.
- Unlit materials, rendered with `materials: "toon"` (3-step ramp plus rim light).
- Secondary motion:
  - hair, ribbons and tail swing on springs;
  - `Wing02_L/R` flap;
  - the 12 `Skirt_0_*` strands are pushed by the thighs.
- No clips: all motion is procedural (IK plus a skipping gait).
